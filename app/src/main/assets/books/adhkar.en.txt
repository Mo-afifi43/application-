# Fortress of the Believer

== Morning and evening remembrance
The Prophet ﷺ taught his companions a set of remembrances to be said after Fajr and after Asr. They are a fortress: whoever says them is protected, and whoever neglects them walks through the day unarmed. Say them with presence, not as a race.
> أَصْبَحْنَا وَأَصْبَحَ المُلْكُ لِلَّهِ، وَالحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ، لا إِلَهَ إِلاَّ اللهُ وَحْدَهُ لا شَرِيكَ لَهُ، لَهُ المُلْكُ وَلَهُ الحَمْدُ وَهُوَ عَلَى كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ.
~ Muslim — "We have entered the morning, and the kingdom belongs to Allah. Praise is for Allah. There is no god but Allah alone, without partner; His is the kingdom and His is the praise, and He is able to do all things." In the evening: "We have entered the evening, and the kingdom has entered the evening belonging to Allah..."
> اللَّهُمَّ بِكَ أَصْبَحْنَا، وَبِكَ أَمْسَيْنَا، وَبِكَ نَحْيَا، وَبِكَ نَمُوتُ، وَإِلَيْكَ النُّشُورُ.
~ Tirmidhi — "O Allah, by You we enter the morning, by You we enter the evening, by You we live, by You we die, and to You is the resurrection." In the evening the ending is: "and to You is the final return."
> بِسْمِ اللهِ الَّذِي لا يَضُرُّ مَعَ اسْمِهِ شَيْءٌ فِي الأَرْضِ وَلا فِي السَّمَاءِ وَهُوَ السَّمِيعُ العَلِيمُ.
~ Abu Dawud and Tirmidhi · three times — "In the name of Allah, with whose name nothing on earth or in heaven can cause harm, and He is the All-Hearing, the All-Knowing." Whoever says it three times will not be struck by sudden calamity.
> رَضِيتُ بِاللهِ رَبًّا، وَبِالإِسْلامِ دِينًا، وَبِمُحَمَّدٍ ﷺ نَبِيًّا.
~ Abu Dawud and Tirmidhi · three times — "I am pleased with Allah as my Lord, with Islam as my religion, and with Muhammad ﷺ as my Prophet."
> حَسْبِيَ اللهُ لا إِلَهَ إِلاَّ هُوَ عَلَيْهِ تَوَكَّلْتُ وَهُوَ رَبُّ العَرْشِ العَظِيمِ.
~ Abu Dawud · seven times — "Allah is sufficient for me; there is no god but He; upon Him I rely, and He is the Lord of the mighty Throne."
> سُبْحَانَ اللهِ وَبِحَمْدِهِ.
~ Muslim · one hundred times — "Glory be to Allah and praise Him." Whoever says it a hundred times in the morning and evening, no one comes on the Day of Judgement with anything better, except one who said the same or more.
- Also recite Ayat al-Kursi once, and Surat al-Ikhlas, al-Falaq and al-Nas three times each.
- And the Master of Seeking Forgiveness (see the chapter on Sayyid al-Istighfar).

== Before sleeping and upon waking
The Prophet ﷺ never slept without remembering Allah. He would blow into his palms after reciting the three protecting surahs and wipe over his body, recite Ayat al-Kursi, and lie on his right side with his hand under his cheek.
> بِاسْمِكَ اللَّهُمَّ أَمُوتُ وَأَحْيَا.
~ Bukhari — "In Your name, O Allah, I die and I live."
> بِاسْمِكَ رَبِّي وَضَعْتُ جَنْبِي وَبِكَ أَرْفَعُهُ، إِنْ أَمْسَكْتَ نَفْسِي فَارْحَمْهَا، وَإِنْ أَرْسَلْتَهَا فَاحْفَظْهَا بِمَا تَحْفَظُ بِهِ عِبَادَكَ الصَّالِحِينَ.
~ Bukhari and Muslim — "In Your name, my Lord, I lay down my side, and by You I raise it. If You take my soul, have mercy on it, and if You release it, protect it as You protect Your righteous servants."
> سُبْحَانَ اللهِ (٣٣) الحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ (٣٣) اللهُ أَكْبَرُ (٣٤)
~ Bukhari and Muslim — "Glory be to Allah (33 times), Praise be to Allah (33 times), Allah is Greatest (34 times)." The Prophet ﷺ taught this to Fatimah and Ali and said it is better for them than a servant.
> اللَّهُمَّ قِنِي عَذَابَكَ يَوْمَ تَبْعَثُ عِبَادَكَ.
~ Abu Dawud and Tirmidhi — "O Allah, protect me from Your punishment on the day You resurrect Your servants."
## Upon waking
> الحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ الَّذِي أَحْيَانَا بَعْدَ مَا أَمَاتَنَا وَإِلَيْهِ النُّشُورُ.
~ Bukhari — "Praise be to Allah who gave us life after He caused us to die, and to Him is the resurrection."
! Sleep is a small death and waking is a small resurrection. Begin and end each day in His name.

== Entering and leaving the home
> بِسْمِ اللهِ، تَوَكَّلْتُ عَلَى اللهِ، وَلا حَوْلَ وَلا قُوَّةَ إِلاَّ بِاللهِ.
~ Abu Dawud and Tirmidhi · when leaving — "In the name of Allah, I place my trust in Allah, and there is no might nor power except with Allah." The Prophet ﷺ said that whoever says this when leaving his house is told: You are guided, protected and sufficed — and Shaytan withdraws from him.
> اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ أَنْ أَضِلَّ أَوْ أُضَلَّ، أَوْ أَزِلَّ أَوْ أُزَلَّ، أَوْ أَظْلِمَ أَوْ أُظْلَمَ، أَوْ أَجْهَلَ أَوْ يُجْهَلَ عَلَيَّ.
~ Abu Dawud, Tirmidhi and Nasa'i · when leaving — "O Allah, I seek refuge in You from going astray or being led astray, from slipping or being caused to slip, from wronging or being wronged, from acting ignorantly or being treated with ignorance."
> بِسْمِ اللهِ وَلَجْنَا، وَبِسْمِ اللهِ خَرَجْنَا، وَعَلَى اللهِ رَبِّنَا تَوَكَّلْنَا.
~ Abu Dawud · when entering — "In the name of Allah we enter, in the name of Allah we leave, and upon Allah our Lord we rely." Then greet your family with the salam.
The Prophet ﷺ said that when a man enters his house and remembers Allah as he enters and as he eats, Shaytan says to his followers: "No place to sleep here and no dinner." But if he enters without remembering Allah, Shaytan says: "You have found a place to sleep," and if he does not remember Allah at his food: "You have found dinner too."
! Make the doorway of your home a place of remembrance, and it becomes a doorway of blessing.

== Before and after eating
> بِسْمِ اللهِ.
~ Bukhari and Muslim — "In the name of Allah." The Prophet ﷺ taught a boy: "Say Bismillah, eat with your right hand, and eat from what is nearest to you."
> بِسْمِ اللهِ فِي أَوَّلِهِ وَآخِرِهِ.
~ Abu Dawud and Tirmidhi — "In the name of Allah, at its beginning and its end." Said if you forget to say Bismillah at the start.
> الحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ الَّذِي أَطْعَمَنِي هَذَا وَرَزَقَنِيهِ مِنْ غَيْرِ حَوْلٍ مِنِّي وَلا قُوَّةٍ.
~ Abu Dawud, Tirmidhi and Ibn Majah — "Praise be to Allah who fed me this and provided it for me without any might or power on my part." Whoever says it after eating, his previous sins are forgiven.
> الحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ حَمْدًا كَثِيرًا طَيِّبًا مُبَارَكًا فِيهِ، غَيْرَ مَكْفِيٍّ وَلا مُوَدَّعٍ وَلا مُسْتَغْنًى عَنْهُ رَبَّنَا.
~ Bukhari — "Praise be to Allah, abundant, pure and blessed praise; a praise that is not enough, that cannot be left, and that cannot be done without, our Lord."
> اللَّهُمَّ بَارِكْ لَهُمْ فِيمَا رَزَقْتَهُمْ، وَاغْفِرْ لَهُمْ، وَارْحَمْهُمْ.
~ Muslim · for the host — "O Allah, bless them in what You have provided them, forgive them, and have mercy on them."
- Eat with the right hand, from what is in front of you, and do not criticise food: the Prophet ﷺ never criticised food; if he liked it he ate it, and if not he left it.
- Allah is pleased with a servant who eats a morsel and praises Him for it, or drinks a sip and praises Him for it.

== Entering the mosque and after the prayer
> اللَّهُمَّ افْتَحْ لِي أَبْوَابَ رَحْمَتِكَ.
~ Muslim · when entering — "O Allah, open for me the gates of Your mercy." Enter with the right foot.
> اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ مِنْ فَضْلِكَ.
~ Muslim · when leaving — "O Allah, I ask You of Your bounty." Leave with the left foot.
## After each obligatory prayer
> أَسْتَغْفِرُ اللهَ (٣) اللَّهُمَّ أَنْتَ السَّلامُ وَمِنْكَ السَّلامُ، تَبَارَكْتَ يَا ذَا الجَلالِ وَالإِكْرَامِ.
~ Muslim — "I seek Allah's forgiveness (three times). O Allah, You are Peace and from You is peace; blessed are You, O Possessor of majesty and honour."
> لا إِلَهَ إِلاَّ اللهُ وَحْدَهُ لا شَرِيكَ لَهُ، لَهُ المُلْكُ وَلَهُ الحَمْدُ وَهُوَ عَلَى كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ، اللَّهُمَّ لا مَانِعَ لِمَا أَعْطَيْتَ، وَلا مُعْطِيَ لِمَا مَنَعْتَ، وَلا يَنْفَعُ ذَا الجَدِّ مِنْكَ الجَدُّ.
~ Bukhari and Muslim — "There is no god but Allah alone, without partner; His is the kingdom and His is the praise, and He is able to do all things. O Allah, none can withhold what You give, none can give what You withhold, and the fortune of the fortunate avails him nothing against You."
> سُبْحَانَ اللهِ (٣٣) الحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ (٣٣) اللهُ أَكْبَرُ (٣٣) ثُمَّ: لا إِلَهَ إِلاَّ اللهُ وَحْدَهُ لا شَرِيكَ لَهُ، لَهُ المُلْكُ وَلَهُ الحَمْدُ وَهُوَ عَلَى كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ.
~ Muslim — Glorify Allah 33 times, praise Him 33 times, magnify Him 33 times, then complete the hundred with the declaration of His oneness. Whoever does so, his sins are forgiven even if they are like the foam of the sea.
> اللَّهُمَّ أَعِنِّي عَلَى ذِكْرِكَ وَشُكْرِكَ وَحُسْنِ عِبَادَتِكَ.
~ Abu Dawud and Nasa'i — "O Allah, help me to remember You, to thank You, and to worship You well." The Prophet ﷺ told Mu'adh: "By Allah, I love you; do not leave this after every prayer."
- Then recite Ayat al-Kursi: whoever recites it after each obligatory prayer, nothing prevents him from entering Paradise except death.
- And the three protecting surahs, once after each prayer, three times after Fajr and Maghrib.

== In distress, worry and fear
> لا إِلَهَ إِلاَّ اللهُ العَظِيمُ الحَلِيمُ، لا إِلَهَ إِلاَّ اللهُ رَبُّ العَرْشِ العَظِيمِ، لا إِلَهَ إِلاَّ اللهُ رَبُّ السَّمَاوَاتِ وَرَبُّ الأَرْضِ وَرَبُّ العَرْشِ الكَرِيمِ.
~ Bukhari and Muslim — "There is no god but Allah, the Mighty, the Forbearing. There is no god but Allah, Lord of the mighty Throne. There is no god but Allah, Lord of the heavens, Lord of the earth and Lord of the noble Throne." The Prophet ﷺ said these words at times of distress.
> لا إِلَهَ إِلاَّ أَنْتَ سُبْحَانَكَ إِنِّي كُنْتُ مِنَ الظَّالِمِينَ.
~ Tirmidhi — "There is no god but You; glory be to You; indeed I have been of the wrongdoers." The prayer of Yunus in the belly of the whale; no Muslim calls upon Allah with it except that He answers him.
> اللَّهُمَّ رَحْمَتَكَ أَرْجُو فَلا تَكِلْنِي إِلَى نَفْسِي طَرْفَةَ عَيْنٍ، وَأَصْلِحْ لِي شَأْنِي كُلَّهُ، لا إِلَهَ إِلاَّ أَنْتَ.
~ Abu Dawud — "O Allah, it is Your mercy that I hope for, so do not leave me to myself even for the blink of an eye, and set right all my affairs. There is no god but You."
> اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنَ الهَمِّ وَالحَزَنِ، وَالعَجْزِ وَالكَسَلِ، وَالبُخْلِ وَالجُبْنِ، وَضَلَعِ الدَّيْنِ وَغَلَبَةِ الرِّجَالِ.
~ Bukhari — "O Allah, I seek refuge in You from worry and grief, from incapacity and laziness, from miserliness and cowardice, from the burden of debt and from being overpowered by men."
> اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي عَبْدُكَ، ابْنُ عَبْدِكَ، ابْنُ أَمَتِكَ، نَاصِيَتِي بِيَدِكَ، مَاضٍ فِيَّ حُكْمُكَ، عَدْلٌ فِيَّ قَضَاؤُكَ، أَسْأَلُكَ بِكُلِّ اسْمٍ هُوَ لَكَ، سَمَّيْتَ بِهِ نَفْسَكَ، أَوْ أَنْزَلْتَهُ فِي كِتَابِكَ، أَوْ عَلَّمْتَهُ أَحَدًا مِنْ خَلْقِكَ، أَوِ اسْتَأْثَرْتَ بِهِ فِي عِلْمِ الغَيْبِ عِنْدَكَ، أَنْ تَجْعَلَ القُرْآنَ رَبِيعَ قَلْبِي، وَنُورَ صَدْرِي، وَجِلاءَ حُزْنِي، وَذَهَابَ هَمِّي.
~ Ahmad — "O Allah, I am Your servant, son of Your servant, son of Your maidservant; my forelock is in Your hand, Your command over me is carried out, Your decree upon me is just. I ask You by every name that is Yours, by which You named Yourself, or revealed in Your Book, or taught to any of Your creation, or kept with Yourself in the knowledge of the unseen: make the Qur'an the spring of my heart, the light of my chest, the remover of my sadness and the reliever of my worry." The Prophet ﷺ said that whoever says it, Allah removes his worry and replaces his sadness with joy.
> حَسْبُنَا اللهُ وَنِعْمَ الوَكِيلُ.
~ Bukhari — "Allah is sufficient for us, and He is the best Disposer of affairs." Said by Ibrahim when thrown into the fire and by Muhammad ﷺ when told that armies had gathered against him.

== The master of seeking forgiveness
The Prophet ﷺ called this supplication Sayyid al-Istighfar, the master of all ways of seeking forgiveness. He said: "Whoever says it during the day with certainty in it and dies that day before evening is of the people of Paradise; and whoever says it at night with certainty in it and dies before morning is of the people of Paradise."
> اللَّهُمَّ أَنْتَ رَبِّي لا إِلَهَ إِلاَّ أَنْتَ، خَلَقْتَنِي وَأَنَا عَبْدُكَ، وَأَنَا عَلَى عَهْدِكَ وَوَعْدِكَ مَا اسْتَطَعْتُ، أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ شَرِّ مَا صَنَعْتُ، أَبُوءُ لَكَ بِنِعْمَتِكَ عَلَيَّ، وَأَبُوءُ بِذَنْبِي، فَاغْفِرْ لِي فَإِنَّهُ لا يَغْفِرُ الذُّنُوبَ إِلاَّ أَنْتَ.
~ Bukhari — "O Allah, You are my Lord; there is no god but You. You created me and I am Your servant, and I am upon Your covenant and promise as much as I am able. I seek refuge in You from the evil of what I have done. I acknowledge Your blessing upon me, and I acknowledge my sin, so forgive me, for none forgives sins except You."
## Why it is the master
Look at its structure: it begins with Tawhid, then acknowledges servitude, then renews the covenant with honesty ("as much as I am able"), then seeks refuge from one's own deeds, then confesses two things side by side — His blessing and my sin — and only then asks, giving the reason: no one else can forgive.
> أَسْتَغْفِرُ اللهَ الَّذِي لا إِلَهَ إِلاَّ هُوَ الحَيُّ القَيُّومُ وَأَتُوبُ إِلَيْهِ.
~ Abu Dawud and Tirmidhi — "I seek the forgiveness of Allah, besides whom there is no god, the Ever-Living, the Sustainer, and I turn to Him in repentance." Whoever says it is forgiven even if he had fled from the battlefield.
! The Prophet ﷺ, whose past and future sins were forgiven, sought forgiveness more than seventy times a day. What about us?

== Supplications from the Qur'an
The best supplications are those Allah Himself taught in His Book. They were the prayers of the prophets and the righteous, and every one of them was answered.
> رَبَّنَا آتِنَا فِي الدُّنْيَا حَسَنَةً وَفِي الْآخِرَةِ حَسَنَةً وَقِنَا عَذَابَ النَّارِ
~ Qur'an 2:201 — "Our Lord, give us good in this world and good in the Hereafter, and protect us from the punishment of the Fire." The supplication the Prophet ﷺ made most often.
> رَبَّنَا لَا تُزِغْ قُلُوبَنَا بَعْدَ إِذْ هَدَيْتَنَا وَهَبْ لَنَا مِن لَّدُنكَ رَحْمَةً ۚ إِنَّكَ أَنتَ الْوَهَّابُ
~ Qur'an 3:8 — "Our Lord, let not our hearts deviate after You have guided us, and grant us mercy from Yourself. Indeed, You are the Bestower."
> رَبِّ اشْرَحْ لِي صَدْرِي ۝ وَيَسِّرْ لِي أَمْرِي ۝ وَاحْلُلْ عُقْدَةً مِّن لِّسَانِي ۝ يَفْقَهُوا قَوْلِي
~ Qur'an 20:25–28 — "My Lord, expand for me my chest, ease my task for me, and untie the knot from my tongue so that they may understand my speech." The prayer of Musa before facing Pharaoh.
> رَبِّ اجْعَلْنِي مُقِيمَ الصَّلَاةِ وَمِن ذُرِّيَّتِي ۚ رَبَّنَا وَتَقَبَّلْ دُعَاءِ ۝ رَبَّنَا اغْفِرْ لِي وَلِوَالِدَيَّ وَلِلْمُؤْمِنِينَ يَوْمَ يَقُومُ الْحِسَابُ
~ Qur'an 14:40–41 — "My Lord, make me an establisher of prayer, and from my descendants. Our Lord, accept my supplication. Our Lord, forgive me and my parents and the believers on the Day the account is established." The prayer of Ibrahim.
> رَبَّنَا هَبْ لَنَا مِنْ أَزْوَاجِنَا وَذُرِّيَّاتِنَا قُرَّةَ أَعْيُنٍ وَاجْعَلْنَا لِلْمُتَّقِينَ إِمَامًا
~ Qur'an 25:74 — "Our Lord, grant us from our spouses and our offspring comfort to our eyes, and make us leaders of the righteous."
> رَبِّ أَوْزِعْنِي أَنْ أَشْكُرَ نِعْمَتَكَ الَّتِي أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيَّ وَعَلَىٰ وَالِدَيَّ وَأَنْ أَعْمَلَ صَالِحًا تَرْضَاهُ وَأَدْخِلْنِي بِرَحْمَتِكَ فِي عِبَادِكَ الصَّالِحِينَ
~ Qur'an 27:19 — "My Lord, enable me to be grateful for Your favour which You have bestowed upon me and upon my parents, and to do righteousness of which You approve, and admit me by Your mercy among Your righteous servants." The prayer of Sulayman.
> رَبِّ زِدْنِي عِلْمًا
~ Qur'an 20:114 — "My Lord, increase me in knowledge."
> حَسْبُنَا اللَّهُ وَنِعْمَ الْوَكِيلُ
~ Qur'an 3:173 — "Sufficient for us is Allah, and He is the best Disposer of affairs."
! Memorise one of these each week and use it in your prostration. You will be praying with the words of the prophets.
