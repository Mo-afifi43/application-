# Gems of Prophetic Wisdom

== On sincerity and the heart
> إِنَّ اللهَ لا يَنْظُرُ إِلَى صُوَرِكُمْ وَأَمْوَالِكُمْ، وَلَكِنْ يَنْظُرُ إِلَى قُلُوبِكُمْ وَأَعْمَالِكُمْ.
~ Abu Hurayrah · Muslim — "Allah does not look at your appearance or your wealth, but He looks at your hearts and your deeds."
People measure one another by looks, titles and bank accounts. Allah measures by the heart: what it loves, what it fears, what it hopes for. A poor man with a sincere heart is closer to Allah than a king whose heart is full of arrogance.
> مَنْ كَانَتِ الآخِرَةُ هَمَّهُ جَعَلَ اللهُ غِنَاهُ فِي قَلْبِهِ، وَجَمَعَ لَهُ شَمْلَهُ، وَأَتَتْهُ الدُّنْيَا وَهِيَ رَاغِمَةٌ، وَمَنْ كَانَتِ الدُّنْيَا هَمَّهُ جَعَلَ اللهُ فَقْرَهُ بَيْنَ عَيْنَيْهِ، وَفَرَّقَ عَلَيْهِ شَمْلَهُ، وَلَمْ يَأْتِهِ مِنَ الدُّنْيَا إِلاَّ مَا قُدِّرَ لَهُ.
~ Anas ibn Malik · Tirmidhi — "Whoever makes the Hereafter his concern, Allah places richness in his heart, gathers his affairs for him, and the world comes to him humbled. Whoever makes the world his concern, Allah places poverty before his eyes, scatters his affairs, and nothing of the world comes to him except what was decreed."
## The lesson
Two people can do the same job and earn the same wage; one is at peace and the other is anxious. The difference is what fills the heart. Put the Hereafter first, and the world falls into its proper place.

== On kindness and mercy
> الرَّاحِمُونَ يَرْحَمُهُمُ الرَّحْمَنُ، ارْحَمُوا مَنْ فِي الأَرْضِ يَرْحَمْكُمْ مَنْ فِي السَّمَاءِ.
~ Abdullah ibn Amr · Tirmidhi and Abu Dawud — "The merciful are shown mercy by the Most Merciful. Be merciful to those on earth, and the One in heaven will be merciful to you."
> مَنْ لا يَرْحَمُ لا يُرْحَمُ.
~ Jarir ibn Abdullah · Bukhari and Muslim — "Whoever does not show mercy will not be shown mercy."
> إِنَّ اللهَ رَفِيقٌ يُحِبُّ الرِّفْقَ، وَيُعْطِي عَلَى الرِّفْقِ مَا لا يُعْطِي عَلَى العُنْفِ.
~ Aisha · Muslim — "Allah is gentle and loves gentleness, and He gives for gentleness what He does not give for harshness."
The Prophet ﷺ kissed his grandchildren, and a Bedouin remarked that he had ten children and had never kissed one of them. The Prophet ﷺ replied: "What can I do for you if Allah has removed mercy from your heart?"
## The lesson
Mercy is not weakness; it is the character of the Most Merciful reflected in His servants. It extends to children, the elderly, servants, animals and even to those who wrong us. Gentleness achieves what harshness never can.

== On the tongue
> إِنَّ العَبْدَ لَيَتَكَلَّمُ بِالكَلِمَةِ مِنْ رِضْوَانِ اللهِ لا يُلْقِي لَهَا بَالاً يَرْفَعُهُ اللهُ بِهَا دَرَجَاتٍ، وَإِنَّ العَبْدَ لَيَتَكَلَّمُ بِالكَلِمَةِ مِنْ سَخَطِ اللهِ لا يُلْقِي لَهَا بَالاً يَهْوِي بِهَا فِي جَهَنَّمَ.
~ Abu Hurayrah · Bukhari — "A servant may utter a word pleasing to Allah, without giving it much thought, by which Allah raises him in rank; and a servant may utter a word displeasing to Allah, without giving it much thought, by which he plunges into Hell."
> المُسْلِمُ مَنْ سَلِمَ المُسْلِمُونَ مِنْ لِسَانِهِ وَيَدِهِ.
~ Abdullah ibn Amr · Bukhari and Muslim — "The Muslim is the one from whose tongue and hand the Muslims are safe."
> أَتَدْرُونَ مَا الغِيبَةُ؟ قَالُوا: اللهُ وَرَسُولُهُ أَعْلَمُ. قَالَ: ذِكْرُكَ أَخَاكَ بِمَا يَكْرَهُ.
~ Abu Hurayrah · Muslim — "Do you know what backbiting is? They said: Allah and His Messenger know best. He said: Mentioning your brother in a way he would dislike."
## The lesson
Words are deeds. They are written, weighed and answered for. Before speaking, three questions: Is it true? Is it kind? Is it necessary? The tongue is small but it can lift a person to Paradise or throw him into the Fire.

== On family and neighbours
> خَيْرُكُمْ خَيْرُكُمْ لأَهْلِهِ، وَأَنَا خَيْرُكُمْ لأَهْلِي.
~ Aisha · Tirmidhi — "The best of you are those who are best to their families, and I am the best of you to my family."
> جَاءَ رَجُلٌ إِلَى رَسُولِ اللهِ ﷺ فَقَالَ: مَنْ أَحَقُّ النَّاسِ بِحُسْنِ صَحَابَتِي؟ قَالَ: أُمُّكَ. قَالَ: ثُمَّ مَنْ؟ قَالَ: أُمُّكَ. قَالَ: ثُمَّ مَنْ؟ قَالَ: أُمُّكَ. قَالَ: ثُمَّ مَنْ؟ قَالَ: أَبُوكَ.
~ Abu Hurayrah · Bukhari and Muslim — "A man came to the Messenger of Allah ﷺ and asked: Who is most deserving of my good companionship? He said: Your mother. He asked: Then who? He said: Your mother. He asked: Then who? He said: Your mother. He asked: Then who? He said: Your father."
> مَا زَالَ جِبْرِيلُ يُوصِينِي بِالجَارِ حَتَّى ظَنَنْتُ أَنَّهُ سَيُوَرِّثُهُ.
~ Aisha and Ibn Umar · Bukhari and Muslim — "Jibril kept advising me about the neighbour until I thought he would make him an heir."
## The lesson
Religion is tested at home before it is tested anywhere else. The Prophet ﷺ helped with housework, played with children, honoured his wives and was generous to neighbours. Goodness that does not reach your own family and neighbours is incomplete.

== On wealth and contentment
> لَيْسَ الغِنَى عَنْ كَثْرَةِ العَرَضِ، وَلَكِنَّ الغِنَى غِنَى النَّفْسِ.
~ Abu Hurayrah · Bukhari and Muslim — "Richness is not having many possessions; richness is the richness of the soul."
> قَدْ أَفْلَحَ مَنْ أَسْلَمَ، وَرُزِقَ كَفَافًا، وَقَنَّعَهُ اللهُ بِمَا آتَاهُ.
~ Abdullah ibn Amr · Muslim — "Successful is the one who has submitted to Allah, is given enough to suffice him, and whom Allah has made content with what He gave him."
> انْظُرُوا إِلَى مَنْ هُوَ أَسْفَلَ مِنْكُمْ، وَلا تَنْظُرُوا إِلَى مَنْ هُوَ فَوْقَكُمْ، فَهُوَ أَجْدَرُ أَنْ لا تَزْدَرُوا نِعْمَةَ اللهِ عَلَيْكُمْ.
~ Abu Hurayrah · Muslim — "Look at those below you and do not look at those above you; that is more likely to keep you from belittling the blessings of Allah upon you."
> مَا نَقَصَتْ صَدَقَةٌ مِنْ مَالٍ.
~ Abu Hurayrah · Muslim — "Charity does not decrease wealth."
## The lesson
Contentment is a wealth that no market can take away. The believer works, earns and gives, but his happiness does not depend on the numbers. He looks at those who have less, thanks Allah, and shares.

== On patience and hope
> عَجَبًا لأَمْرِ المُؤْمِنِ، إِنَّ أَمْرَهُ كُلَّهُ خَيْرٌ، وَلَيْسَ ذَاكَ لأَحَدٍ إِلاَّ لِلْمُؤْمِنِ: إِنْ أَصَابَتْهُ سَرَّاءُ شَكَرَ فَكَانَ خَيْرًا لَهُ، وَإِنْ أَصَابَتْهُ ضَرَّاءُ صَبَرَ فَكَانَ خَيْرًا لَهُ.
~ Suhayb · Muslim — "How wonderful is the affair of the believer: all of it is good, and that is for no one but the believer. If good comes to him he is grateful, and that is good for him; if hardship comes to him he is patient, and that is good for him."
> مَا يُصِيبُ المُسْلِمَ مِنْ نَصَبٍ وَلا وَصَبٍ وَلا هَمٍّ وَلا حُزْنٍ وَلا أَذًى وَلا غَمٍّ، حَتَّى الشَّوْكَةِ يُشَاكُهَا، إِلاَّ كَفَّرَ اللهُ بِهَا مِنْ خَطَايَاهُ.
~ Abu Sa'id and Abu Hurayrah · Bukhari and Muslim — "No fatigue, illness, worry, sadness, harm or grief befalls a Muslim, not even a thorn that pricks him, except that Allah wipes out some of his sins by it."
> إِنَّ عِظَمَ الجَزَاءِ مَعَ عِظَمِ البَلاءِ، وَإِنَّ اللهَ إِذَا أَحَبَّ قَوْمًا ابْتَلاهُمْ، فَمَنْ رَضِيَ فَلَهُ الرِّضَا، وَمَنْ سَخِطَ فَلَهُ السَّخَطُ.
~ Anas · Tirmidhi — "The greatness of the reward is with the greatness of the trial. When Allah loves a people He tests them; whoever is content receives His pleasure, and whoever is displeased receives His displeasure."
> لا يَمُوتَنَّ أَحَدُكُمْ إِلاَّ وَهُوَ يُحْسِنُ الظَّنَّ بِاللهِ.
~ Jabir · Muslim — "Let none of you die except thinking well of Allah."
## The lesson
Pain is never wasted in the life of a believer. Every difficulty is a purification, and every patient breath is written as reward. Hold on, think well of your Lord, and wait for the ease that always follows hardship.
