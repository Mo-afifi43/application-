# Purification of the Heart

== Sincerity: the root of every deed
Ikhlas means to do a deed for Allah alone, seeking nothing from anyone else — not praise, not reputation, not even the good feeling of being seen as religious. It is the difference between a tree with roots and a tree cut and planted in sand: both look the same for a day.
> وَمَا أُمِرُوا إِلَّا لِيَعْبُدُوا اللَّهَ مُخْلِصِينَ لَهُ الدِّينَ
~ Qur'an 98:5 — "And they were not commanded except to worship Allah, being sincere to Him in religion."
The Prophet ﷺ warned of "the minor shirk", and when asked what it was, he said: showing off. On the Day of Judgement, Allah will say to those who worshipped for people's eyes: "Go to those you were showing off to, and see if you find any reward with them."
## How to grow sincerity
- Hide some of your good deeds: a charity no one knows about, a prayer in the night, a fast no one sees.
- When praised, remember your faults and say: "O Allah, do not take me to account for what they say, and forgive me what they do not know."
- Do not abandon a deed for fear of showing off — that is what Shaytan wants. Do it, and correct the intention.
- Ask yourself before, during and after: for whom?
! Sincerity is a struggle that never ends. The scholars said: nothing was harder for me than my intention, because it keeps turning over.

== Repentance: the door that never closes
Tawbah means to return. Every human being drifts; the believer is the one who keeps coming back. Allah loves those who repent, and the Prophet ﷺ himself sought forgiveness more than seventy times a day, though he had no sins.
> إِنَّ اللَّهَ يُحِبُّ التَّوَّابِينَ وَيُحِبُّ الْمُتَطَهِّرِينَ
~ Qur'an 2:222 — "Indeed, Allah loves those who constantly repent and loves those who purify themselves."
Sincere repentance has three parts: leaving the sin, regretting it, and resolving never to return. If the sin involved the rights of others, a fourth: returning what was taken or seeking their forgiveness.
Do not wait to be perfect before you return. The Prophet ﷺ described a man who killed a hundred people and then sincerely set out for a land of righteousness, and died on the way — and the angels of mercy took him because his heart was closer to where he was going than to where he came from.
## Practical steps
- Repent immediately after a slip; do not let it become a habit.
- Follow the sin with a good deed; it wipes it out.
- Never say "I have repented so many times, it is useless." Every return is accepted, and the One you return to does not tire.
- Keep company with people who remind you of Allah.
! Allah is more pleased with the repentance of His servant than a traveller who finds his lost camel in the desert with all his food and water on it.

== Patience: half of faith
Sabr is to hold the soul steady: patient in obeying Allah, patient in staying away from sin, and patient with what Allah decrees. The scholars said patience is half of faith, and gratitude is the other half.
> يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا اسْتَعِينُوا بِالصَّبْرِ وَالصَّلَاةِ ۚ إِنَّ اللَّهَ مَعَ الصَّابِرِينَ
~ Qur'an 2:153 — "O you who believe, seek help through patience and prayer. Indeed, Allah is with the patient."
Patience is not silence with a boiling heart; it is a calm that comes from knowing that the One who decreed this is wise and merciful. The Prophet ﷺ said that true patience is at the first blow — at the moment the news arrives, when the heart is most tempted to rebel.
When his son Ibrahim died in his arms, the Prophet ﷺ wept and said: "The eye weeps and the heart grieves, but we say only what pleases our Lord." Tears are not against patience; complaining about Allah is.
## The three fields of patience
- Patience in worship: keeping the prayer when you are tired, the fast when you are hungry, the truth when it costs.
- Patience against sin: turning the eye away, closing the app, walking out of the gathering.
- Patience with decree: illness, loss, delay, and the trials of raising children and living with people.
! Whoever is patient, Allah gives him patience, and no one has been given a gift better and wider than patience.

== Gratitude: the way to more
Shukr is to recognise a blessing, attribute it to Allah, and use it in what pleases Him. A tongue that praises, a heart that acknowledges, and limbs that obey — that is complete gratitude.
> لَئِن شَكَرْتُمْ لَأَزِيدَنَّكُمْ
~ Qur'an 14:7 — "If you are grateful, I will surely increase you."
The Prophet ﷺ stood in prayer at night until his feet swelled. Aisha asked why, when Allah had forgiven him everything. He replied: "Should I not be a grateful servant?" Gratitude was the motive of the greatest worship, not fear.
Most people are blind to the blessings they have and fixated on the one thing they lack. The cure is to count: sight, hearing, breath, faith, family, safety, food, and the fact that you are reading these words while millions cannot read.
## A daily practice
- Every morning, say "Alhamdulillah" for three specific things — different ones each day.
- When a blessing comes, say "Masha Allah, la quwwata illa billah" and share some of it.
- Thank people; whoever does not thank people does not thank Allah.
- Use each blessing in obedience: a healthy body that prays, a mind that learns, wealth that gives.

== Trust in Allah: the rest of the heart
Tawakkul is to depend on Allah with the heart while working with the hands. A Bedouin asked the Prophet ﷺ whether he should tie his camel or trust in Allah. He said: "Tie it, and trust in Allah."
> وَمَن يَتَوَكَّلْ عَلَى اللَّهِ فَهُوَ حَسْبُهُ
~ Qur'an 65:3 — "And whoever relies upon Allah, then He is sufficient for him."
The Prophet ﷺ said that if people relied on Allah as He deserves, He would provide for them as He provides for the birds: they leave in the morning hungry and return in the evening full. Notice that the birds leave the nest. Trust is not laziness; it is working without anxiety.
Anxiety comes from believing that results depend on us. Tawakkul frees the heart: you do your part, and the outcome belongs to the One who is Wise. Whatever He chooses is better for you than what you would have chosen for yourself, if you could see the whole picture.
## Signs of tawakkul
- You plan carefully but sleep peacefully.
- You do not envy, because provision is distributed by Him.
- You do not fear people's disapproval when you are upon the truth.
- After doing your best, you can say sincerely: "Hasbunallahu wa ni'mal wakil" — Allah is sufficient for us, and He is the best Disposer of affairs.

== Humility: the beauty of the believer
Humility is to know your true size before Allah and to treat people accordingly: not to look down on anyone, and not to reject the truth when it comes from someone you consider beneath you. The Prophet ﷺ defined arrogance as exactly these two things: rejecting truth and despising people.
> وَعِبَادُ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الَّذِينَ يَمْشُونَ عَلَى الْأَرْضِ هَوْنًا
~ Qur'an 25:63 — "And the servants of the Most Merciful are those who walk upon the earth gently."
The Prophet ﷺ said: "No one humbles himself for the sake of Allah except that Allah raises him." He sat where the gathering ended, ate with the poor, greeted children, answered the invitation of a slave, and rode a donkey. A stranger entering his gathering could not tell which one was the Prophet.
The first sin in creation was arrogance — "I am better than him." Whenever you feel the words "I am better" forming in your heart, know whose voice that is.
## Practices
- Serve people with your own hands: carry, clean, cook, help.
- Accept correction gracefully, even from a child.
- Remember your origin (a drop of fluid) and your end (a body in the earth).
- Greet first, and give the salam to those you do not know.

== Remembrance: the life of the heart
Dhikr is the food of the heart. The Prophet ﷺ compared the one who remembers his Lord and the one who does not to the living and the dead. When you say "Subhanallah", "Alhamdulillah", "La ilaha illa Allah", "Allahu Akbar", you are speaking the words most beloved to Allah, and the angels carry them up.
> الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا وَتَطْمَئِنُّ قُلُوبُهُم بِذِكْرِ اللَّهِ ۗ أَلَا بِذِكْرِ اللَّهِ تَطْمَئِنُّ الْقُلُوبُ
~ Qur'an 13:28 — "Those who believe and whose hearts find rest in the remembrance of Allah. Unquestionably, by the remembrance of Allah hearts find rest."
Remembrance is not limited to the tongue. Reading Qur'an is remembrance; reflecting on creation is remembrance; obeying a command because you remembered Allah is the highest remembrance. But the tongue is the doorway, and it should never be idle.
A man complained to the Prophet ﷺ that the laws of Islam were many and asked for something to hold on to. He said: "Let your tongue stay moist with the remembrance of Allah."
## Making it a habit
- Guard the morning and evening remembrances; they are a fortress around your day.
- Fill the dead moments — walking, waiting, driving — with tasbih and istighfar.
- Say "Bismillah" before every act and "Alhamdulillah" after it.
- Remember Allah in the gathering, so that the gathering is not a regret on the Day of Judgement.

== Love of Allah: the highest station
Everything in this book leads here. The one who is sincere, repentant, patient, grateful, trusting, humble and remembering is a person who loves Allah — and the one who loves Allah finds worship sweet, sin bitter and the world small.
> وَالَّذِينَ آمَنُوا أَشَدُّ حُبًّا لِّلَّهِ
~ Qur'an 2:165 — "But those who believe are stronger in love for Allah."
The Prophet ﷺ described three things by which a person tastes the sweetness of faith: that Allah and His Messenger are more beloved to him than anything else, that he loves a person only for Allah, and that he would hate to return to disbelief as he would hate to be thrown into fire.
How is love of Allah grown? By knowing Him through His names, by reading His words as a letter written to you, by remembering His gifts, by doing what He loves and staying near those who love Him. Love follows knowledge and grows with company.
> قُلْ إِن كُنتُمْ تُحِبُّونَ اللَّهَ فَاتَّبِعُونِي يُحْبِبْكُمُ اللَّهُ
~ Qur'an 3:31 — "Say: If you love Allah, then follow me; Allah will love you."
The proof of love is following the Beloved's Messenger. And the reward of love is love: the servant draws near with voluntary deeds until Allah loves him — and then He guides his hearing, his sight, his hand and his foot.
! Ask Allah every day with the Prophet's prayer: "O Allah, I ask You for Your love, the love of those who love You, and the love of every deed that brings me closer to Your love."
