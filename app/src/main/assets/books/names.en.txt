# The Beautiful Names of Allah

== Why learn His Names?
> إِنَّ لِلَّهِ تِسْعَةً وَتِسْعِينَ اسْمًا، مِائَةً إِلاَّ وَاحِدًا، مَنْ أَحْصَاهَا دَخَلَ الجَنَّةَ.
~ Abu Hurayrah · Bukhari and Muslim — "Allah has ninety-nine names, one hundred less one; whoever enumerates them will enter Paradise."
To "enumerate" the names is more than memorising a list. It is to know their meanings, to worship Allah through them and to call upon Him by them, as the Qur'an commands:
> وَلِلَّهِ الْأَسْمَاءُ الْحُسْنَىٰ فَادْعُوهُ بِهَا
~ Qur'an 7:180 — "And to Allah belong the most beautiful names, so call upon Him by them."
You cannot love what you do not know. Many people worship Allah with fear alone, or ask Him only when in need, because they have not met Him through His names. When you learn that He is al-Wadud, the Loving, your prayer changes. When you learn that He is al-Latif, the Subtly Kind, you begin to see His gentle hand in the details of your life.
This book gathers a selection of the names in five families: mercy and love, majesty and power, knowledge, forgiveness, and provision. Each name is followed by what it means for the way we live.
! Choose one name each week. Repeat it, reflect on it, and call upon Allah by it in your prostration.

== Mercy and love: Ar-Rahman, Ar-Rahim, Al-Wadud
## Ar-Rahman — the Most Merciful
Ar-Rahman describes a mercy so vast that it reaches every creature: the believer and the disbeliever, the bird in its nest and the fish in the sea. Every breath, every heartbeat and every drop of rain is a share of this mercy. The Prophet ﷺ said that Allah divided mercy into a hundred parts; He sent one part to the earth, by which all creatures show compassion to one another, and kept ninety-nine for the Day of Resurrection.
## Ar-Rahim — the Especially Merciful
Ar-Rahim is the mercy Allah gives specially to those who believe: guidance, forgiveness, the sweetness of faith and Paradise. Ar-Rahman is about who He is; Ar-Rahim is about what He does for those who turn to Him.
## Al-Wadud — the Loving
> وَهُوَ الْغَفُورُ الْوَدُودُ
~ Qur'an 85:14 — "And He is the Forgiving, the Loving."
Allah loves His righteous servants and makes them beloved to the hearts of others. His love is not distant admiration; He loves the repentant, the patient, the just and those who purify themselves. Knowing this name turns worship from duty into a relationship.
## Living by these names
Be merciful to those beneath you, and you will taste the mercy of the One above you. Ask Allah by His mercy in every hardship — it is wider than any sin and nearer than any fear.

== Majesty and power: Al-Malik, Al-Aziz, Al-Qadir
## Al-Malik — the King
Kings on earth own a little for a while. Allah owns everything, always. Every atom is in His kingdom; every heart is between two of His fingers. When you understand that He is the King, you stop fearing those who only appear to hold power.
> قُلِ اللَّهُمَّ مَالِكَ الْمُلْكِ تُؤْتِي الْمُلْكَ مَن تَشَاءُ وَتَنزِعُ الْمُلْكَ مِمَّن تَشَاءُ
~ Qur'an 3:26 — "Say: O Allah, Owner of all sovereignty, You give sovereignty to whom You will and You take sovereignty away from whom You will."
## Al-Aziz — the Almighty
Al-Aziz is the One whom nothing can overcome and whose honour cannot be touched. The Qur'an pairs this name with al-Hakim (the Wise) again and again, teaching that His power is never arbitrary; it is always guided by wisdom.
## Al-Qadir — the All-Capable
Nothing is difficult for Him. He creates from nothing, gives life to bones that have turned to dust, and changes a situation in a moment. Whoever knows al-Qadir does not say "impossible" about anything that Allah has promised.
## Living by these names
Seek honour from Allah alone; whoever seeks it from people is humiliated. When the way seems blocked, remember that you are asking the One who says "Be" and it is.

== Knowledge: Al-Alim, As-Sami, Al-Basir
## Al-Alim — the All-Knowing
Allah's knowledge encompasses the past, the present and the future; what is hidden in the depths of the sea and what is hidden in the depths of the heart. Not a leaf falls without His knowledge.
> وَمَا تَسْقُطُ مِن وَرَقَةٍ إِلَّا يَعْلَمُهَا
~ Qur'an 6:59 — "Not a leaf falls but that He knows it."
## As-Sami — the All-Hearing
He hears every voice at once, in every language, without confusion: the complaint of a woman in Madinah whom Aisha could not hear across the room, the whisper of a supplication under the blanket, the silent cry of a heart that has no words.
## Al-Basir — the All-Seeing
He sees the black ant on a black rock in a dark night. He sees the tear you hide, the effort no one credited, and the sin no one witnessed. This name is both a comfort and a warning.
## Living by these names
Never feel alone: He hears you. Never feel unseen: He sees you. And never feel safe in a sin because no one knows — the One who knows is the only One who matters. Ihsan is born from these three names.

== Forgiveness: Al-Ghafur, At-Tawwab, Al-Afuw
## Al-Ghafur — the All-Forgiving
Ghafr in Arabic means to cover and protect. Allah covers the sins of His servant so that they are not exposed, and protects him from their consequences. He forgives the great and the small, the repeated and the ancient, for whoever turns to Him.
> قُلْ يَا عِبَادِيَ الَّذِينَ أَسْرَفُوا عَلَىٰ أَنفُسِهِمْ لَا تَقْنَطُوا مِن رَّحْمَةِ اللَّهِ ۚ إِنَّ اللَّهَ يَغْفِرُ الذُّنُوبَ جَمِيعًا
~ Qur'an 39:53 — "Say: O My servants who have transgressed against themselves, do not despair of the mercy of Allah. Indeed, Allah forgives all sins."
## At-Tawwab — the Accepter of Repentance
Allah turns to His servant twice: first He inspires him to repent, then He accepts the repentance. Every sincere return is welcomed, however many times it is repeated. The Prophet ﷺ said that Allah is more joyful at the repentance of His servant than a man who finds his lost camel in the desert with all his provisions on it.
## Al-Afuw — the Pardoner
Afw goes beyond forgiveness: it erases the sin as if it never happened. This is the name Aisha was taught to call upon in the Night of Power: "O Allah, You are Pardoning and You love to pardon, so pardon me."
## Living by these names
Repent immediately and often; the door never closes before the last breath. And pardon others — whoever pardons is pardoned by the Pardoner.

== Provision and kindness: Ar-Razzaq, Al-Wahhab, Al-Karim, Al-Latif
## Ar-Razzaq — the Provider
Every provision — food, health, knowledge, family, faith — comes from Him. He provides the bird that leaves its nest hungry and returns full. Knowing this name frees the heart from anxiety about tomorrow and from envy of what others have.
> إِنَّ اللَّهَ هُوَ الرَّزَّاقُ ذُو الْقُوَّةِ الْمَتِينُ
~ Qur'an 51:58 — "Indeed, it is Allah who is the Provider, the Firm Possessor of Strength."
## Al-Wahhab — the Bestower
A gift that expects nothing in return is a hibah. Allah gives without being asked, gives more than is asked, and gives to those who do not deserve it. Zakariyya called upon Him by this name and was given a son in old age.
## Al-Karim — the Most Generous
His generosity is not measured by our worth. He rewards a good deed ten to seven hundred times, He forgives without being reminded, and He is shy to return the hands of His servant empty when they are raised to Him.
## Al-Latif — the Subtly Kind
Al-Latif knows the finest details and reaches His servant with kindness through ways he never expected. Yusuf, thrown into a well and sold as a slave, was carried by this kindness to the throne of Egypt and said: "Indeed, my Lord is Subtly Kind to whom He wills."
## Living by these names
Work for your provision without worrying about it; it was written before you were born. Ask the Bestower boldly. Be generous, for Allah loves the generous. And when life seems to turn against you, trust that al-Latif is arranging something you cannot yet see.
