# Worship Made Easy

== Purification and wudu
Islam begins its worship with cleanliness. The Prophet ﷺ said that purity is half of faith, and no prayer is accepted without it. There are two levels: wudu, the minor ablution for prayer, and ghusl, the full bath after major impurity, such as intimacy or the end of a woman's period.
## How to perform wudu
- Make the intention in your heart and say Bismillah.
- Wash both hands up to the wrists three times.
- Rinse the mouth three times, and draw water into the nose and blow it out three times.
- Wash the face three times, from the hairline to the chin and from ear to ear.
- Wash the right arm up to and including the elbow three times, then the left.
- Wipe the head once with wet hands, from the front to the back and returning, then wipe the ears inside and out.
- Wash the right foot up to and including the ankle three times, then the left.
- Finish by saying: "I bear witness that there is no god but Allah alone, without partner, and that Muhammad is His servant and Messenger."
> مَنْ تَوَضَّأَ فَأَحْسَنَ الوُضُوءَ خَرَجَتْ خَطَايَاهُ مِنْ جَسَدِهِ حَتَّى تَخْرُجَ مِنْ تَحْتِ أَظْفَارِهِ.
~ Muslim — "Whoever performs wudu well, his sins leave his body, even from beneath his fingernails."
## What breaks wudu
Anything that exits from the private parts (urine, stool, wind), deep sleep, loss of consciousness, and — according to many scholars — touching the private parts directly. Doubt does not break wudu: if you are unsure, you remain pure.
! Keep the sequence and do not waste water. The Prophet ﷺ performed wudu with less than a litre.

== How to pray, step by step
Prayer is the meeting of the servant with his Lord. Learn it correctly once, and it becomes the most natural act of your day.
- Face the qiblah (the direction of the Ka'bah), stand with calm, and make the intention for the specific prayer.
- Raise your hands to the shoulders or ears and say "Allahu Akbar" (the opening takbir). Place the right hand over the left on the chest.
- Recite the opening supplication if you wish, then "A'udhu billahi min ash-shaytan ir-rajim" and "Bismillah ir-Rahman ir-Rahim", then Surat al-Fatihah, then any other passage of the Qur'an in the first two units.
- Say "Allahu Akbar" and bow (ruku') with a straight back, hands on the knees, saying "Subhana Rabbiyal Adhim" three times.
- Rise saying "Sami'a Allahu liman hamidah", and when upright: "Rabbana wa lakal hamd."
- Say "Allahu Akbar" and prostrate (sujud) on seven points: forehead with the nose, both palms, both knees and the toes of both feet, saying "Subhana Rabbiyal A'la" three times.
- Sit up saying "Allahu Akbar", say "Rabbighfir li", then prostrate a second time.
- Rise for the second unit and repeat. After the second prostration of the second unit, sit for the tashahhud.
> التَّحِيَّاتُ لِلَّهِ وَالصَّلَوَاتُ وَالطَّيِّبَاتُ، السَّلامُ عَلَيْكَ أَيُّهَا النَّبِيُّ وَرَحْمَةُ اللهِ وَبَرَكَاتُهُ، السَّلامُ عَلَيْنَا وَعَلَى عِبَادِ اللهِ الصَّالِحِينَ، أَشْهَدُ أَنْ لا إِلَهَ إِلاَّ اللهُ وَأَشْهَدُ أَنَّ مُحَمَّدًا عَبْدُهُ وَرَسُولُهُ.
~ The tashahhud · Bukhari and Muslim — "All greetings, prayers and good words are for Allah. Peace be upon you, O Prophet, and the mercy of Allah and His blessings. Peace be upon us and upon the righteous servants of Allah. I bear witness that there is no god but Allah, and I bear witness that Muhammad is His servant and Messenger."
- In prayers of three or four units, stand after this and complete them, then sit for the final tashahhud, adding the prayer upon the Prophet ﷺ: "Allahumma salli ala Muhammad wa ala ali Muhammad, kama sallayta ala Ibrahim wa ala ali Ibrahim, innaka Hamidun Majid; Allahumma barik ala Muhammad..."
- Make any supplication you wish, then turn your face to the right saying "As-salamu alaykum wa rahmatullah", and then to the left.
! Pray with tranquillity. The Prophet ﷺ told a man who prayed hurriedly to repeat his prayer three times, then taught him to be still in every position.

== The five prayers and their times
Allah prescribed five prayers a day and night, each at a time that marks a turning point of the day. Praying at the earliest part of the time is most beloved to Allah.
- Fajr: two units, from true dawn until sunrise. Before it, two voluntary units that the Prophet ﷺ never abandoned.
- Dhuhr: four units, from when the sun passes its highest point until an object's shadow equals its length.
- Asr: four units, from then until sunset; it should be prayed before the sun turns yellow. The Prophet ﷺ said that whoever misses Asr, it is as if his family and wealth were taken from him.
- Maghrib: three units, from sunset until the red twilight disappears.
- Isha: four units, from the disappearance of the twilight until midnight, and in need until dawn.
> حَافِظُوا عَلَى الصَّلَوَاتِ وَالصَّلَاةِ الْوُسْطَىٰ وَقُومُوا لِلَّهِ قَانِتِينَ
~ Qur'an 2:238 — "Guard strictly the prayers, especially the middle prayer, and stand before Allah in devout obedience."
A traveller may shorten the four-unit prayers to two and combine Dhuhr with Asr, and Maghrib with Isha. A sick person prays sitting, or lying down if necessary; the prayer is never dropped while the mind is present.
If a prayer is missed through sleep or forgetfulness, it is prayed as soon as one remembers. Praying in congregation multiplies the reward twenty-seven times, and the mosque is the best place for men to pray, while the home is a place of blessing for the prayer of both men and women.

== Friday prayer
Friday is the best day on which the sun rises: Adam was created on it, and on it the Hour will come. It is the weekly gathering of the Muslims, and its prayer replaces Dhuhr for adult men who are residents and able.
> يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا إِذَا نُودِيَ لِلصَّلَاةِ مِن يَوْمِ الْجُمُعَةِ فَاسْعَوْا إِلَىٰ ذِكْرِ اللَّهِ وَذَرُوا الْبَيْعَ
~ Qur'an 62:9 — "O you who believe, when the call is made for the prayer on Friday, hasten to the remembrance of Allah and leave trade."
## The Sunnah of Friday
- Take a bath, use the siwak, put on your best clothes and perfume.
- Go early; the angels record those who arrive first, and close their registers when the imam sits.
- Pray voluntary units until the imam begins, then listen to the two sermons in complete silence — even saying "be quiet" to someone during the sermon cancels the reward.
- Pray two units behind the imam.
- Recite Surat al-Kahf on Friday; it is a light between the two Fridays.
- Send abundant blessings upon the Prophet ﷺ; your blessings are presented to him.
- Seek the hour of acceptance: there is a moment on Friday in which no Muslim asks Allah for something good except that He gives it — many scholars said it is the last hour before Maghrib.
Whoever abandons three Fridays out of negligence, the Prophet ﷺ warned, Allah seals his heart. Women, travellers and the sick are excused, and if they attend it is accepted from them.

== Fasting Ramadan
Fasting is obligatory on every adult, sane Muslim who is able. It begins at true dawn and ends at sunset, and it is one of the pillars of Islam.
## Who is excused
- The sick and the traveller fast on other days instead.
- Pregnant and nursing women who fear harm make up the days later.
- The elderly and the chronically ill who cannot fast feed a poor person for each day.
- Women during menstruation and post-natal bleeding do not fast, and make up the days afterwards.
## What breaks the fast
Eating or drinking deliberately, intimacy, deliberate vomiting and menstruation. Whoever eats or drinks by forgetfulness continues his fast; Allah has fed him.
## What does not break it
Using the siwak, rinsing the mouth without swallowing, bathing, tasting food without swallowing, and unintentional dust or smoke.
## The Sunnah of fasting
> تَسَحَّرُوا فَإِنَّ فِي السَّحُورِ بَرَكَةً.
~ Bukhari and Muslim — "Take the pre-dawn meal, for in it there is blessing."
- Delay the suhur and hasten the iftar; break the fast with fresh dates, then dry dates, then water, saying: "The thirst has gone, the veins are moistened, and the reward is confirmed, if Allah wills."
- Guard the tongue and eyes; a fast that does not restrain the limbs is only hunger.
- Pray the night prayer (Tarawih); whoever stands Ramadan in faith and seeking reward is forgiven.
- Seek Laylat al-Qadr in the odd nights of the last ten; it is better than a thousand months.
- Give zakat al-fitr — a measure of staple food for every member of the household — before the Eid prayer.

== Zakat
Zakat is the third pillar: a purification of wealth that is a right of the poor, not a charity from the rich. It is due on wealth that reaches the nisab and has been possessed for a full lunar year.
## On what and how much
- Gold, silver, cash and savings: 2.5% each year, once they reach the nisab of about 85 grams of gold or its value.
- Trade goods: 2.5% of their market value.
- Crops and fruits: 10% if watered by rain, 5% if irrigated at cost, at harvest.
- Livestock: according to fixed tables for camels, cattle and sheep.
There is no zakat on the house you live in, the car you drive, your tools or your personal belongings.
## Who receives it
> إِنَّمَا الصَّدَقَاتُ لِلْفُقَرَاءِ وَالْمَسَاكِينِ وَالْعَامِلِينَ عَلَيْهَا وَالْمُؤَلَّفَةِ قُلُوبُهُمْ وَفِي الرِّقَابِ وَالْغَارِمِينَ وَفِي سَبِيلِ اللَّهِ وَابْنِ السَّبِيلِ
~ Qur'an 9:60 — "Zakat is only for the poor, the needy, those who administer it, those whose hearts are to be reconciled, for freeing captives, for those in debt, in the cause of Allah, and for the stranded traveller."
Zakat is not given to one's own parents, children or spouse, whose support is already an obligation, and it is not given to the wealthy. It is best given to relatives in need, then the neighbours, then the poor of one's own town.
! Calculate your zakat on a fixed date each year, and consider it not a loss but the most profitable investment: Allah multiplies it and blesses what remains.

== Hajj and Umrah
Hajj is obligatory once in a lifetime for every Muslim who is adult, sane, free and able — physically, financially, and with a safe road. Umrah is a shorter visit that can be made at any time of the year.
## Umrah
- Enter ihram at the miqat: bathe, men wear two white unsewn cloths, women wear ordinary modest clothing, and say: "Labbayk Allahumma Umrah."
- Perform tawaf: seven circuits of the Ka'bah beginning at the Black Stone, then pray two units behind the Station of Ibrahim.
- Perform sa'i: seven laps between Safa and Marwah.
- Shave or shorten the hair. The Umrah is complete.
## Hajj: the days
- 8 Dhul-Hijjah: enter ihram for Hajj and go to Mina, praying there.
- 9 Dhul-Hijjah: the day of Arafat — the pillar of Hajj. Stand in supplication until sunset, then go to Muzdalifah and spend the night.
- 10 Dhul-Hijjah: stone the large pillar, offer the sacrifice, shave or shorten, then perform the tawaf of Hajj and the sa'i.
- 11–13 Dhul-Hijjah: stay in Mina and stone the three pillars each day after midday. Before leaving Makkah, perform the farewell tawaf.
> لَبَّيْكَ اللَّهُمَّ لَبَّيْكَ، لَبَّيْكَ لا شَرِيكَ لَكَ لَبَّيْكَ، إِنَّ الحَمْدَ وَالنِّعْمَةَ لَكَ وَالمُلْكُ، لا شَرِيكَ لَكَ.
~ The talbiyah — "Here I am, O Allah, here I am. Here I am; You have no partner; here I am. Indeed, all praise, blessing and sovereignty are Yours; You have no partner."
During ihram one does not cut hair or nails, use perfume, hunt, or engage in intimacy, and men do not cover their heads or wear sewn garments. Above all, the pilgrim guards his tongue and temper: "Whoever performs Hajj without obscenity or sin returns as the day his mother bore him."

== Voluntary prayers and the night prayer
The obligatory prayers are the foundation; the voluntary prayers fill their gaps and raise the servant in rank. On the Day of Judgement, if something is missing from the obligatory prayers, Allah says: "See if My servant has any voluntary prayers to complete them with."
## The regular Sunnah prayers
The Prophet ﷺ said that whoever prays twelve voluntary units each day, Allah builds him a house in Paradise: two before Fajr, four before and two after Dhuhr, two after Maghrib, and two after Isha.
## Witr
Witr is the seal of the night: one, three or more odd units after Isha, before sleeping or at the end of the night prayer. The Prophet ﷺ never left it, even when travelling.
## The night prayer
> كَانُوا قَلِيلًا مِّنَ اللَّيْلِ مَا يَهْجَعُونَ ۝ وَبِالْأَسْحَارِ هُمْ يَسْتَغْفِرُونَ
~ Qur'an 51:17–18 — "They used to sleep but little of the night, and in the hours before dawn they would ask forgiveness."
Tahajjud is the honour of the believer. In the last third of the night, Allah descends in a manner befitting His majesty to the lowest heaven and says: "Who is calling upon Me, that I may answer him? Who is asking Me, that I may give him? Who is seeking My forgiveness, that I may forgive him?" Two units in that hour, with the world asleep, are worth more than many in the day.
## Duha
Two to eight units after the sun has risen well, before midday. The Prophet ﷺ said they suffice for the charity owed by every joint of the body each morning.
! Start with what you can keep up: two units before Fajr, witr before sleeping. The most beloved deeds to Allah are the consistent ones, even if small.
